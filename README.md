# AdrenalineJunkie 

**Are you an Adrenaline Junkie?**

Do you live for the thrill of battle? The rush of a perfectly timed dodge? The glorious chaos of being surrounded by enemies while your Viking makes increasingly questionable life decisions?
Then why does your adrenaline disappear the moment things get interesting?!

**AdrenalineJunkie** puts you in control of your adrenaline habit.

Whether you just want to take the edge off vanilla's aggressive decay or turn your Viking into a caffeine-fueled combat enthusiast, the choice is yours.

## Get Your Fix

Three simple configuration options let you fine-tune your adrenaline experience:
- **Adrenaline Gain:** Earn adrenaline faster or slower. Because sometimes one near-death experience just isn't enough.
- **Adrenaline Decay:** Control how quickly adrenaline drains. Keep the rush going long after the screaming stops.
- **Decay Delay:** Adjust how long adrenaline sticks around before it starts disappearing. Savor the moment!

## The Default Dose
AdrenalineJunkie ships with settings designed to make adrenaline feel more rewarding without completely throwing balance out the window.

| Setting | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Gain Multiplier | 1.25x | 25% faster adrenaline gain |
| Decay Multiplier | 0.50x | 50% slower adrenaline decay |
| Decay Delay | 1.50x | 50% longer before decay begins |

Want vanilla behavior? Set everything to `1.0`.
Want to experiment? Go nuts. It's your Viking, your adrenaline, and your questionable decisions.

## Features

- Fully configurable adrenaline gain, decay, and decay delay
- Preserves Valheim's original adrenaline curves and progression
- Doesn't change maximum adrenaline or activation thresholds
- Lightweight and client-side
- Works on dedicated servers without requiring server installation
- No unnecessary bells, whistles, or suspiciously glowing potions

## Multiplayer

AdrenalineJunkie is a client-side mod. Each player can configure their own settings, and the dedicated server does not need the mod installed.
For a consistent multiplayer experience, we recommend agreeing on settings with your fellow Vikings.

**AdrenalineJunkie: Because the only thing that should run out faster than your adrenaline is your supply of healing meads.**

*Side effects may include excessive confidence, reckless charging, and suddenly believing you can solo an Ashlands fortress while screaming "LEEEEERRROOOOOY!"*
